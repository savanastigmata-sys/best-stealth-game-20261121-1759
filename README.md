Nightshade Gallery — a top-down stealth game in three wings. You are Wren, a shadow courier: steal ten Moonlit Relics across the Moonlit Gallery, the Ember Archive and the Star Vault, slip past sixteen lantern guards with vision cones you can read at a glance, hide behind display plinths and inside shade-ferns, throw pebbles to lure a guard away, and leave by the green door unseen. Patrols you can learn, a detection meter that gives you a chance to recover, footsteps you can hear and see, a score for every relic, and a Ghost rating for a run no guard ever noticed.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, E to take a relic, Q to throw a pebble, click to walk somewhere. No network, no build step, no libraries — just the files under game/.
